# Practice Engine

## Purpose

Turn a meaningful real-life situation into a structured practice.

## Core model

```text
EVENT
↓
THOUGHT
↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓
LEARNING
```

```text
Situation

↓

↓

↓

```

## Example exercise types

### Replay
Recreate the triggering situation and identify feelings and thoughts.

### Become the Observer
Notice the thought without immediately believing it or acting on it.

### Different perspectives
Explore what another person might be experiencing.

### Pause
Practise creating space before responding.

### Choice
Explore possible responses and what each might lead to.

## Evolution

A practice can evolve across multiple days as the user learns. The user can save it, modify it, pause it, complete it, or turn it into a 10-day challenge.
