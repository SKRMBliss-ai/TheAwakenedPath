# Product Principles

## Core principles

- The user is training, not being fixed.
- The product is a personal practice gym for the mind.
- Practice should connect to real life.
- When something is stuck, bring it into the gym.
- Bespoke Practice Rooms should be interactive training environments, not pages of advice.
- Progress measures practice rather than morality or a purported psychological score.
- The user retains control over saving, modifying, pausing, completing, or changing a practice.
- The AI Practice Coach should help explore and practise; it should not diagnose.
- Tolle/Singer-inspired ideas should function as design philosophy rather than repeated authority claims.
- Kids and adults need distinct experiences.
- Prefer reusable capabilities over one-off screens.

## Product north star

> Don't build another screen. Build another capability of the Practice Gym.

## Engineering principle

One reusable Practice Room engine should support many situations through structured content/configuration rather than separate implementations for every room.
