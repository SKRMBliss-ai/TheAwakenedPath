# Design System

## Experience split

### Kids Gym

The experience should be:

- Visual
- Playful
- Character-led
- Story-based
- Interactive
- Simple
- Safe
- Experiential

Desired child feeling:

> “I am learning how my mind works.”

### Adult Gym

The experience should feel:

- Sophisticated
- Calm
- Private
- Personalised
- Practice-oriented

## Home experience

The proposed adult entry point is:

**YOUR MIND GYM**

> How are you arriving today?

- 😊 I'm good
- 😐 I'm okay
- 😟 Something is bothering me
- 🔥 I'm really struggling
- 🌱 I just want to practise

The experience should respond differently to each state. When someone is really struggling, the system should slow down, offer grounding/supportive options, and avoid immediately introducing an intense exercise.
