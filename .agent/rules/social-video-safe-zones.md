# Social Video Safe Zones — Text & Graphic Placement

## Rule
Never place text, subtitles, logos, or key graphics in the bottom 25% of a vertical video frame (9:16 ratio). On YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels, that region is covered by the platform UI: video title, channel name, like/share/comment buttons, and the progress bar.

## Safe text zone
- **Top boundary**: no higher than ~8% from top (leaves room for the top UI bar on some platforms)
- **Bottom boundary**: no lower than 25% from bottom (~75% mark of the frame height)
- Keep main title text and subtitles between 15% and 70% of frame height.

## Specific to SHITABITO / SKRMBLISS content
- The white lion title card ("SHITABITO · THE LUMINOUS LION") should sit in the vertical centre or upper half.
- Any subtitle / quote / tagline: place it just below the main title — never drift toward the bottom third.
- Lower-third style text is a standard TV convention; it does not work on short-form vertical video. Avoid it entirely.
- The SKRMBliss watermark / URL can stay near the bottom only if it is small enough to read even if partially obscured — it is branding, not content.

## Shots already published
Do not change shots already live on YouTube — platform edits can affect SEO/timestamps. Apply this rule only to drafts and unpublished shots going forward.
