# Curriculum

## Kids Gym

For children approximately 5–10, the experience is visual, playful, character-led, story-based, interactive, simple, safe, and experiential.

### Practice Rooms

- Feelings Room — recognise emotions.
- Thought Room — discover what the mind is saying.
- Body Detective Room — notice where feelings show up physically.
- Pause Room — practise creating space before reacting.
- Different Story Room — explore how the same event can feel different depending on the story the mind creates.
- Friendship Room — practise difficult friendship situations.
- Anger Room — practise what to do when anger takes over.
- Worry Room — practise meeting worry without letting it run everything.
- Kindness Room — practise kind actions and words.
- Reflection Room — look back and discover what was learned.

## Adult Gym

The adult experience is more sophisticated, calm, and private.

### Two entry paths

1. **I have something on my mind** — bring a specific real-life situation into the gym.
2. **I want to grow** — receive a varied daily practice when nothing specific is bothering the user.

## Ten-Day Mind Strength Challenge

1. Notice
2. Observe
3. Separate
4. Feel
5. Pause
6. Perspective
7. Choice
8. Release
9. Real Life
10. Integrate
